# Privacy — Tester Summary

*Last updated: October 16, 2026. This is a plain-language summary for testers. It is not legal advice.*

## The tester signup form collects your email only

When you sign up for the closed test with the [signup form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSe9xwqTplppsbXFboRZ43DdTWTdFVZUpScbXGt7jL6VBu_v5g/viewform), we collect **one thing: your email address.** (The form is a Google Form, so Google also processes it under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).)

- **Why:** only to add you to the Google Play closed test and send you the install link.
- **Not sold, not shared** for advertising, and not used for marketing unrelated to the test.
- **Delete on request:** you can ask us to delete your email at any time (see below). Deleting it may remove you from the test.
- **Please don't** post your email, or any private details, in a public GitHub issue or discussion.

## How to ask for deletion or a copy of your data

Open a [Tester signup help issue](https://github.com/binkybell225/BluEmpress/issues/new/choose) titled "Data request" **without writing your email in it**, or reply to any message you received from us about the test. We will tell you privately how to confirm it is you, and then delete your email from the tester list.

## What the app does with information

The app has its own **[Privacy Policy](https://bluempress-lzezh6wzea-uc.a.run.app/play/privacy.html)** and **[Terms](https://bluempress-lzezh6wzea-uc.a.run.app/play/terms.html)**. In short (the app policy is the official version):

- What you type in the chat is sent to the app's server and then to Google's Gemini models so BluEmpress can reply.
- The server keeps a short in-memory conversation for your session; it is dropped when the server restarts.
- Preferences are kept on your device. "Clear this device" in Settings removes them.
- If you tap **Report** on a reply, the reason and an optional short snippet are stored in a server log.
- The app is for adults 18 and older.

## This GitHub repository

GitHub processes your username, comments, and any content you post here under [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). The landing page uses **no cookies, no analytics, and no trackers**.

Everything you post in issues, pull requests, and discussions is public. Do not include private information.
