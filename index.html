What is BluEmpress?

BluEmpress is an Android app where you talk to BluEmpress, a fictional AI queen character with a New Orleans-inspired voice and a royal-blue-and-gold style. Open the BluRoom, chat, and watch her mood and portrait change with the conversation. She is entertainment, not a real person.

Features

💬 Chat

Talk to BluEmpress in the BluRoom. Replies come from Google's Gemini models.

🎭 9 moods

Regal, Playful, Confident, Arrogant, Sassy, Calm, Mischievous, Focused, and BluRoom, each with its own portrait.

🎚️ 5 chat modes

Regal, Focused, Playful, Guarded, and BluRoom change her tone and length.

💙 BluRoom Check

A rare random moment where her eyes and crown glow and she resets the energy with classy sass.

🧠 Memory you control

Temporary, long-term, and user-controlled memories you can review and export.

🗂️ Studio desk

Tasks, reminders, notes, goals, and projects to chat about.

🎙️ Voice

Hold to speak and auto-read replies with your phone's text-to-speech.

⚡ Quick actions

One-tap starters for brainstorming, writing, planning, worldbuilding, and more.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. No payment is needed, and none is promised for testing.

Who can join?

Adults 18 and older with an Android device and a Google account.

Why 14 days?

Google Play asks closed-test apps to be tested by real people for 14 days in a row.

Is BluEmpress a real person?

No. She is a fictional AI character, not a therapist, doctor, lawyer, or emergency contact.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not an App Store app. iPhone users can use the web version with Add to Home Screen; ask in Discussions.

Is the app open source?

No. The app and brand are all rights reserved. Only the JSON in /data is open for suggestions under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.

How do I report a bug or give feedback?

Open an issue. Please never post your email.
